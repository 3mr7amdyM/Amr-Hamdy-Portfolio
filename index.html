System Overview
A summary of scope, geography, and current focus.

I lead end-to-end IoT device integration — from onboarding and configuration through full lifecycle management — for platforms spanning smart mobility, smart lighting, waste management, and critical infrastructure monitoring. My work sits at the intersection of hardware, connectivity, and cloud: designing solutions that hold up in the field, not just in the spec sheet.

Over nine years I've moved from embedded hardware design (PCB, FPGA, RTOS) into large-scale platform integration — building the connective tissue between physical sensors and the dashboards city operators actually use. Recent work spans Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 smart city initiatives, including port logistics with ZATCA and infrastructure monitoring with STC.

Currently based in Riyadh, working as Engineering Lead – IoT Integrated Devices at IoT Squared.
